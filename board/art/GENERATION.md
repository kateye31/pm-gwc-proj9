# Quest council illustration

Asset: `quest-council.png`.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then copied into this project. The source paintings were inspected as conceptual references; they were not edited. The scene is illustrative, not a portrait of the actual students or a confirmed game design.

Final prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide illustrated banner for a playful collaborative party-game-style meeting dashboard called Knight's Escape, no lettering. Primary request: an original richly illustrated medieval quest council: four young women adventurers gathered round a parchment map of a three-room castle, discussing and sketching their own adventure. A small friendly dragon curls around the table edge, golden keys, hand-drawn route marks, quill, simple wooden objects. Style: sophisticated editorial gouache and screen-printed storybook illustration, bold chunky ink contours, expressive silhouettes, tactile paper grain and irregular shapes, playful sophisticated party game art direction, not photorealism, not generic vector icons. Refer conceptually to illuminated medieval manuscripts and dramatic dragon paintings but make a fresh friendly scene. Composition: panoramic 3:2 landscape, table and characters grouped centrally and toward right, dark uncluttered upper-left area for HTML heading overlay; detailed expressive people and illustrated objects, strong readable shapes at small sizes. Palette: ink brown #1a1410, dark wood #2a1f18, rusty orange #7a2e06, parchment cream #f6e8cf, torch yellow #ffc21a, moss green #c2ba52. Mood: warm lantern-lit castle studio, collaborative mischievous energy. Constraints: no text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no watermark. This is a finished raster illustration for a website.
