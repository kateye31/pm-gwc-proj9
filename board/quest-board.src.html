      Project charter

      Ship a playable 4-chamber Java escape room and demo it live on Thursday, Nov 12.

      Done means

      • A new player escapes all 4 chambers in under 15 minutes
      • Every puzzle has two hints and an explanation
      • 4 playtesters outside the team tried it before Nov 9
      • Every student can explain the part they built

      In scope

      • Knight select, 4 chambers, escape screen
      • Computer browsers only

      Out of scope

      • Phones and tablets
      • Accounts, saved progress, leaderboards
