Project gallery

Devpost shows these after the cover, in this order. All are 1200 × 800 and come from the merged game on branch 1.0 and the Meet the creators page. The files are in gallery-final/. Paste each caption into the image’s caption box.

1. Choose your knight

Sir Byte, Dame Loop or Sir Null, then a difficulty. The castle door is Tvishaa’s drawing.

2. Every chamber opens on her drawing

Each chamber starts with Tvishaa’s full drawing and its name. Chamber 1 is LabyrinthLearn, named by Leslie.

3. LabyrinthLearn: classes and objects

Answer the code question to pick the right direction. A wrong turn tells you exactly what you mixed up.

4. The Alchemy Lab: methods and encapsulation

Guess what each potion’s code prints. Right answers bubble green and spell “Squire doing king’s work.”

5. The Hall of Illusions: inheritance

Three look-alike doors, each with code on it. Pick a wrong one and it fades into the wall.

6. The Final Door: keys are flashcards

Answer the warlock in your own words. Each key fills a keyhole and gives you a flashcard to remember.

7. You escaped

The weak-spot scroll tells you which chamber to review. Then play again.

8. Works on phones

No install. Open it in any browser.
